Four things, before any button

  1. Buying the coin is permanent — GNOT flows one way and is burned; there is no sell-back.
  2. Staked coin comes back 1×, win or lose; only the reward rides on the verdict. (The GNOT that minted the coin was already burned — that part was spent, not staked.)
  3. The reward is conviction — stake × time on the winning side — drawn from newly-minted coin, capped so it can never be farmed.
  4. Anyone may answer (with a bond; seasoned courts reserve the first day for qualified answerers), anyone but the answerer may dispute, and anyone may flag; bonds spend real tokens with no undo — staking is the one act you can unwind, until an answer posts.
